About This Course

ITCS333 covers the full stack of internet software development: HTML5, CSS3, JavaScript (ES6+), PHP 8, and MySQL. By the end of the semester students will be able to design and deploy complete, database-driven web applications.

  • Credit hours: 3
  • Lab hours: 2 per week
  • Prerequisite: ITCS214 or equivalent
